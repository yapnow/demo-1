Most apps help you scroll past people. YapNow helps you actually talk to them.

We're building a place for duplex conversation — real, two-way talk between two people who chose what they care about. Tell us your language, where you are, what you do and what you love. We find someone who fits, and you're talking before the moment passes.

Chat and voice are free to start. Video is our premium experience. Each medium has its own matchmaking, tuned to how people talk there.

How it works

From sign-up to “hello” in four steps.

  1. 01

    Sign up & verify

    Create your account and verify with a quick face and voice check, so everyone you meet is real.

  2. 02

    Set your preferences

    Pick location, language, profession, passions and gender preference from ready-made tags.

  3. 03

    Tap Connect

    Choose Chat, Call or Video. Matchmaking searches everyone available who fits what you picked.

  4. 04

    Start talking

    You're paired instantly. If your connection drops, YapNow reconnects you automatically.
